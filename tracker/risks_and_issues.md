# Risks and issues

## Risks

| Id | Risk | Impact | Response | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R01 | No Zurich sandbox is available | No environment | Waitlist for at most 7 days, then decision D01 | Sandbox owner |
| R02 | The sandbox is deleted after 10 days without a sign-in | Work lost | Weekly sign-in reminder, backup pack in Stage 6, recovery plan | Sandbox owner |
| R03 | SAM Professional activation fails | Stage 1 blocked | Retry order in Stage 1 troubleshooting | Builder |
| R04 | The Software Asset Workspace does not install | Main screen missing | Fix steps in Stage 1, classic menus as fallback | Builder |
| R05 | A product shows wrong counts after the license check | Wrong results | Check Matching Discovery Models, edition, product type and processor fields (Stages 2 to 4 troubleshooting) | Builder |
| R06 | No removal candidates appear | Reclamation story incomplete | Fallback rule and evidence path in Stage 5 troubleshooting | Builder |
| R07 | The visitor login can change something | Data can be damaged | Ten visitor tests in Stage 6 before it is switched on | Builder |
| R08 | A secret or real data reaches GitHub | Data exposure | Rules in docs/09, automatic secret scan on every upload | Everyone |
| R09 | The case study claims more than was shown | Credibility | Every claim links to a screenshot; acceptance checklist in Stage 7 | Reviewer |
| R10 | Sessions slip and the project drags on | Momentum lost | Stages sized for one session each; the tracker shows the next step | Everyone |
